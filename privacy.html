Privacy policy

photos-bridge is a personal tool used only by its owner.

It uploads photos to Google Photos accounts that the owner connects, and it reads storage quota from Google Drive. It reads only media that it uploaded.

Tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's private server. No data is shared with or sold to any third party.

To revoke access, remove the app at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
